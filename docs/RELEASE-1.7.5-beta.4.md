# VE Builder Card 1.7.5-beta.4

## English

- Show total daily charged energy above a full-width stacked solar/grid bar in Costs and savings.
- Colour-coded bar segments and a compact legend show kWh and percentages.
- Missing source readings leave the unallocated fraction empty. Tiny rounding differences are normalised when all sources are known.
- The existing instantaneous power display (W) remains unchanged. Existing dashboards and entity selections remain compatible.
- Test in Home Assistant before approving a stable release.
